# 08 — About Page Experience & Storytelling

**Phase 6.** Approved. The About page only.

> Supersedes the About section list in `05-site-architecture.md`.

---

## Objective

The About page is not a company biography, a timeline, or a page of
achievements. It answers one question:

> **"Why should I trust Ascent Studios with my personal brand?"**

Visitors leave with confidence in the company's **thinking, philosophy and
process** — not knowing when it was founded.

## Core philosophy

People don't buy because a company exists. **People buy because they believe the
company understands them.**

This page builds emotional trust through clarity, philosophy and conviction. It
should feel like a conversation between founders, not a corporate profile.

## The five things a visitor should think

1. They genuinely understand founders.
2. They have a structured methodology.
3. They care about business outcomes.
4. They aren't another content agency.
5. I want to work with these people.

## Emotional journey

```
Curiosity → Understanding → Alignment → Trust → Confidence → Conversation
```

## 01 — Hero

**Purpose:** introduce Ascent with confidence. **Do not begin by talking about
the company. Begin with a belief.**

Visitors immediately understand what Ascent stands for.

**Layout:** large editorial headline · supporting copy · minimal imagery ·
extensive whitespace.

**Motion — L3.** Kinetic typography is appropriate here: a single belief
statement revealed deliberately. Not the L4 hero treatment used on the homepage —
this page opens quieter, because it is arguing rather than introducing.

**Success:** visitors immediately understand this page is about **ideas, not
history**.

> **Note on the narrative label.** The stated arc opens with *Who We Are*, but
> this section's brief says not to talk about the company. Both are satisfied by
> a belief statement: what a company chooses to assert first *is* who it is. Do
> not add a separate "who we are" block to satisfy the label.

**Must not:** open with a founding date, team photo, or company description ·
use a stock office image.

---

## 02 — Why We Exist

**Purpose:** explain why Ascent was created. **Do not tell a founder story for
its own sake.** Focus on the market problem and why founders deserve a better
approach.

**Layout:** editorial, minimal, typography-first.

**Motion — L2.** Restrained.

**Success:** visitors understand the motivation behind Ascent.

**Must not:** become an origin story · list credentials · describe a career
history.

---

## 03 — The Problem With Traditional Agencies

**Purpose:** position **the market, not competitors**. Explain the industry's
shortcomings without attacking individual agencies.

The shortcomings:

- Chasing vanity metrics
- Producing content without strategy
- Measuring likes instead of business outcomes
- Treating every founder the same

**Tone: respectful and professional.** This is the same discipline the homepage
uses — attack the model, never the vendor. The visitor hired one of these
agencies; making them feel foolish for it produces defensiveness, and
defensiveness is the enemy of recognition.

**Motion — L2.** Restrained. Recognition is damaged by decoration.

**Success:** visitors recognise the limitations of conventional approaches.

**Must not:** name or imply specific agencies · use a competitor comparison
table · adopt a superior tone.

---

## 04 — Our Belief

**Purpose:** present Ascent's core belief. Introduce the **Personal Brand
Operating System**. Explain why content alone is never the answer, and position
the operating system as **the missing layer**.

**Motion — L3.** The pivot of the page. Worth a section-level reveal.

**Success:** visitors understand what truly differentiates Ascent.

**Must not:** repeat the homepage §04 diagram · list the three pillars as
services · become a product pitch.

---

## 05 — Our Philosophy

**Purpose:** communicate the principles that guide every engagement.

- Strategy before production
- Systems before campaigns
- Business outcomes before vanity metrics
- Long-term brand equity over short-term attention
- Consistency over occasional virality

**Avoid buzzwords. Write with clarity.** Each of these is a stated preference
with a rejected alternative attached — that structure is why they read as
principles rather than slogans. Keep it.

**Motion — L2.** Staggered reveal, five items, inside the six-sibling cap.

**Success:** visitors feel aligned with Ascent's thinking.

**Must not:** exceed six principles · use abstractions without a rejected
alternative.

---

## 06 — How We Think

**Purpose:** reveal the **decision-making framework** behind Ascent. Show that
every recommendation follows a structured process.

**Avoid discussing deliverables. Focus on methodology.**

**Motion — L3.**

**Success:** visitors believe Ascent has a repeatable process.

> ### The most important recommendation on this page
>
> §04, §05 and §06 are three consecutive sections of thinking — roughly a third
> of the page spent in abstraction. That is a real risk: a founder scanning at
> speed will experience it as three variations of the same paragraph.
>
> **The fix is that §06 should demonstrate rather than declare.** Walk through
> one real decision — a positioning call, a channel choice, a piece of content
> that was killed and why. A worked example is concrete, it breaks the
> abstraction, and it proves the framework exists in a way that describing the
> framework cannot.
>
> This is also the section that most directly earns the page's stated goal:
> *they have a structured methodology*. Declaring a methodology is what every
> agency does. Showing one is the differentiator.

**Must not:** list deliverables · describe the framework only in the abstract ·
repeat §05's principles in different words.

## 07 — How We Work

**Purpose:** reuse the four-stage engagement model from the homepage. Slightly
more context, but **concise**.

```
Discovery & Audit → Strategy & Positioning → Production → Optimize
```

This reinforces consistency across the site — a visitor arriving here after the
homepage should recognise the model instantly, not re-learn it.

**Motion — L2.** No pinning available, and that is correct. The homepage version
is the full progressive-reveal treatment; this is the reference version. The
difference in weight is what signals which page owns the explanation.

**Success:** visitors understand how an engagement progresses.

**Must not:** exceed the homepage version in depth · re-draw the loop diagram ·
introduce a fifth stage.

## 08 — Who We Build For

**Purpose:** define the ideal client so visitors self-qualify.

Startup founders · SaaS companies · CEOs · high-growth personal brands.

> ⚠️ **Audience list mismatch.** Phase 6 lists five entries including *business
> owners*; the final homepage §07 lists four, without it. The same audience
> described two ways across two pages is the kind of inconsistency a founder
> notices.
>
> **Recommendation: the four-item list everywhere.** *Business owners* is the
> vaguest of the five and the least aligned with a positioning built
> specifically for founders and CEOs — it widens the funnel at exactly the point
> the page is meant to narrow it. Applied as four here; reinstate on one word if
> you want it.

**Motion — L2.** Simple.

**Success:** the right audience immediately recognises themselves.

## 09 — Why It Works

**Purpose:** summarise the philosophy and bring the page together. Emphasise
that **strategy, production and metrics are parts of one operating system — not
isolated services.** Transition naturally into the CTA.

**Motion — L3.**

### The loop's third appearance

The operating-system loop now appears on three pages. Each must do a different
job, or it becomes repetition rather than reinforcement:

| Page | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **Home §04** | **Asserted** — the circular diagram. The visual centre of the site. |
| **Services §06** | **Explained** — the mechanism described in full |
| **About §09** | **Concluded** — a typographic summary that closes an argument |

**Do not re-draw the circular diagram here.** One circle per page, and this
page's circle would be its third-best. §09 earns its version of the loop by
having spent the whole page building to it — the words are enough.

**Must not:** re-draw the loop · introduce new information · read as a second
Services page.

## Content principles

**Never describe Ascent as** a creative agency · a social media agency · a
content factory · a marketing agency.

**Always reinforce:** Ascent Studios builds **Personal Brand Operating Systems**
for founders.

## Visual principles

Editorial · calm · minimal · thoughtful.

Favour typography and whitespace over decorative graphics. Motion and imagery
support the story; they never dominate it.

Given §04–§06 are already abstract, this page is the strongest candidate on the
site for **process artefacts as imagery** — a real positioning document, a real
content calendar. They are visual, they are concrete, and they are evidence.

## Internal linking

Natural pathways only: **learn about our Services** · **book a strategy call** ·
**send a WhatsApp message**.

Avoid unrelated navigation paths.

Per Phase 4, About → Services is the primary onward step. A visitor who has just
been convinced by the philosophy is asking *what exactly do I get* — send them
there, not back to Home.

## Resolved by this phase

Phase 4 flagged that *What We Believe*, *Our Philosophy* and *How We Think* were
three belief-adjacent sections that would collapse into the same page said three
times. Phase 6 separates their remits, close to the proposed split:

| Section | Remit |
|---|---|
| **04 Our Belief** | What Ascent believes about the market — and the operating system as the answer |
| **05 Our Philosophy** | The operating principles that belief produces |
| **06 How We Think** | The reasoning method applied to a specific founder |

The split holds. The residual risk is density, not duplication — see the
recommendation under §06.

Also changed from the Phase 4 draft: *The Future We're Building* is **removed**,
replaced by §09 *Why It Works*. A better ending — it closes the argument the
page has been making rather than opening a new one immediately before the CTA.
