# 07 — Services Page

**Approved content.** The three pillars, defined.

Answer one question: **what exactly do we get?**

Do not present services as disconnected offerings. Present them as **one
integrated operating system**.

# 03 — Content Strategy

> Clear positioning, audience insight, and content direction built around what
> the market cares about. **Delivered in 1–2 sessions.**

**What's included:**

- Audience and market research
- Positioning
- Content pillars and ICP definition

### Notes

*Delivered in 1–2 sessions* is doing real work. It answers the founder's
unspoken "how much of my time does this cost" at the exact moment they are
imagining a long discovery process. Keep it prominent — it should not be
demoted to small print.

---

# 04 — Production

> Your expertise, transformed into sharp, consistent content across platforms
> and formats.
>
> **Deliverables are scoped to your strategy, not a fixed package** — the exact
> mix depends on where the strategy says attention should go.

**What's included:**

- Short-form video, and long-form video filmed and edited
- Carousels
- Long-form written content — blogs, newsletters, ghostwritten pieces
- Website design and SEO
- Repurposing across formats, complete with a content calendar

### Notes

**"Scoped to your strategy, not a fixed package" is the most important sentence
on this page.** It is the line that proves the system claim: a package is a
deliverable, and a deliverable is exactly what failed these founders before.
Everything else on the page is evidence; this sentence is the argument.

It should sit *above* the deliverables list, not below it. A founder who reads
the list first sees a menu; a founder who reads the sentence first sees a
system, and then reads the list as its range.

> **Flag — Website design and SEO.** This is the one item that does not
> obviously read as content production, and it risks looking like an unrelated
> service bolted onto the pillar. It fits if framed as the owned destination
> the content drives toward — the place demand lands. Worth a line of framing in
> the copy phase rather than leaving it as a bullet among video formats.

---

# 05 — Metrics

> Content measured by meaningful outcomes like leads generated, conversations,
> and demand.

**What we track:**

- Leads generated
- Conversations started
- Demand signals

> **Not views, not likes** — only the numbers that tell you the system is
> working.

### Notes

This section is the direct answer to the founder's original complaint: *"I got
more content, I didn't get more business."* It carries more emotional weight
than the other two pillars and should be given room accordingly.

"Not views, not likes" is the sharpest sentence in the whole services offer. It
is a refusal, and refusals read as confidence.

# 06 — How the three connect

> Strategy sets the direction. Production runs it. Metrics tells you whether
> it's working — **and feeds back into the strategy.**

### Notes

This is the most important section on the page and the reason it is not a
service menu. The feedback arrow is the entire differentiator: three services
sold together are still three services, and the loop is what makes them a
system.

The same loop appears on the homepage at §04. Here it is **explained**; there it
is **asserted**. Do not simply reprint the homepage diagram — this is where the
mechanism gets described.

## Ideal Client — altitude

Home §07 *Who We Work With* asks **am I the right kind of founder?**
Services §07 *Ideal Client* asks **is my business at the right stage for this
engagement?**

Different questions. Keep them distinct so the pages do not compete.

## FAQ — altitude

Home FAQ: *should I even have this conversation?* — positioning, fit, outcomes.
Services FAQ: *how does delivery work?* — cadence, ownership of output,
revisions, onboarding, turnaround, tooling.

## CTA

Specified for this page: **"Book a Call"**.

> ⚠️ **Inconsistency to resolve.** Every other page uses **"Book a Strategy
> Call"**, and Phase 4 requires the Connect With Us block to be identical
> everywhere. Two labels for the same action weakens a signature element and
> makes analytics harder to read.
>
> **Recommendation: "Book a Strategy Call" site-wide.** *Strategy* qualifies the
> visitor before they click and sets the expectation that this is a working
> conversation, not a sales call — which is exactly the filtering this site
> exists to do. "Book a Call" is shorter but invites lower-intent enquiries, and
> the target is ten *qualified* calls a month.
>
> Applied as specified until you decide. One label, everywhere, either way.

## Motion

No pinned sections. Phase 3 caps pinning at two site-wide and both are allocated
to the homepage (§04 and §06). The Services page carries Level 1–3 motion only.

§06 *How the three connect* is the natural candidate for a scroll-linked
explanation of the loop — scrubbable, legible at every position, and readable
without motion.

---

## What this page settles

The five proposed OS modules — Positioning, Narrative, Production, Distribution,
Demand — are **retired**. They were a working model pending confirmation. The
real structure is three pillars with defined inclusions, and it is better:
Content Strategy, Production, Metrics, closing into a loop.

Nothing else in the system depended on the five names.
